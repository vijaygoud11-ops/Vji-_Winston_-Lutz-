Research / QA prototype — not validated for clinical acceptance. Confirm field and BB overlays visually for every image. This tool measures projected 2D offsets, not a reconstructed 3D radiation isocenter. TIFF decoding requires internet access to load a library; PDF printing uses Chrome. Uncompressed DICOM only; verify overlays and calibration.

1. Calibration and image settings

Field center is detected from exposed intensity, not prescribed field size. Calibration: pixel size at isocenter = detector pixel size × SAD / SID. Use the actual EPID calibration; do not rely on assumed values.

2. Analysis settings

Choose the TIFF or DICOM tab, select 1 to 8 images, and assign gantry / collimator / couch angles in the image table. Analyze the selected images to get individual 2D errors and combined statistics. The radiation field center is detected from image intensity and field edges; no nominal field size is required.

Combined error is reported as RMS of the successfully analyzed 2D offsets, plus mean and maximum. It is NOT a 3D isocenter sphere or 3D Winston–Lutz reconstruction.
